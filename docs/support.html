Support

Let’s get you back to your path.

Contact support

Open a Purrgress support issue on GitHub. Include the app version, iOS version, what happened and what you expected.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post your goal text, private screenshots, receipts, Apple Account details or payment information. Describe the issue without personal content.

I already bought the app.

The current version is a paid download. Check that you are using the same Apple Account, then redownload it from your purchase history. The upcoming free-first-World version will preserve existing paid core access and offer Restore Purchases.

I want to request a refund.

Apple handles payments and refunds. Visit Apple Report a Problem with the account used to pay. Do not share your receipt in a public issue.

My World is missing.

Current progress is saved locally. Removing the app or resetting its data can remove local progress; cloud backup and cross-device sync are not promised. Before troubleshooting, keep the installed app and avoid clearing its data.

AI suggestions are unavailable.

Check your network connection. You can edit local suggestions to match your goal. Do not assume a fallback suggestion was produced by AI. Read about AI data handling.

Reminders are not appearing.

Check Purrgress notification permission in iOS Settings and your reminder time window. Focus modes and system notification settings can affect delivery.
