How it works

Give one small goal a walkable path.

A World is one goal. A Spot is one small action. You do the action, confirm it, and see your cat move forward.

1. Pick something real.

Choose a goal you can describe, such as “clear one corner of my desk.” Purrgress holds one current World, rather than asking you to manage a second collection of projects.

2. Edit the path to fit your day.

Suggestions are a starting point. Change the words, add or remove steps, and arrange the path before you begin. AI suggestions need a network connection and send your goal to the AI provider; local fallback steps remain editable.

Three example paths

These are example inputs and edits, not guaranteed AI output or customer testimonials.

Clear one corner of my desk

  1. Put the mug in the sink.
  2. Move three loose items to their places.
  3. Clear one hand-sized patch.

Put away some clothing

  1. Choose three items from the pile.
  2. Fold or hang those three items.
  3. Put them where they belong.

Start a draft

  1. Open the document.
  2. Write one sentence about the idea.
  3. Leave a note for the next paragraph.

3. Confirm your own progress.

After doing a real action, mark its Spot as explored. The cat, sound and progress display respond to your confirmation. The app does not detect whether you performed the real-world action.

4. Come back to the same path.

The current World and progress are saved on your device. You can use reminders and record a completed World. Cross-device sync is not part of the current product promise.
