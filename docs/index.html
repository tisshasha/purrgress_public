What is Purrgress?

Purrgress is a native iPhone and iPad app that turns real goals into a sequence of small, editable steps, with a retro cat companion. It saves your current World and progress on your device, with reminders and completed records. It works on one current World at a time.

Who is it for?

People who like gentle visual feedback and want help choosing a concrete first action for everyday goals. It is not a medical treatment or a promise that an action will get done.

Is Purrgress free?

The currently released app is a paid download: US $19.99. A free-first-World version with a US $29.99 one-time unlock is being prepared; it is not live yet. See the version-specific details.

Does the cat know when I finish something?

You confirm each action yourself. Purrgress does not automatically monitor your real-world activity.

Can I use it offline?

Editing your saved path and confirming progress use local data. AI suggestions require an internet connection; local editable suggestions are available when the remote service is unavailable.

Does AI see my goal?

When the current app requests AI suggestions, the entered goal is sent to DeepSeek. Progress and completed records are stored locally. Read the actual data flow.
