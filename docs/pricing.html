Free experience
One complete, editable World; up to 10 Spots; one successful AI plan when the new AI service is ready.
One-time unlock
Local core planning for new Worlds, one current World at a time; no automatic renewal.
AI allowance
The planned unlock includes 30 additional successful AI plans once. This is not an unlimited or recurring allowance; manual planning stays available.
Keep your progress

Already paid?
Existing paid-download customers will retain their purchased core access. They will not be asked to buy the same core features again.

What does “lifetime” mean?

One purchase permanently unlocks the app’s local core features, with no renewal. It does not mean unlimited cloud AI or a guarantee that third-party online services will run forever.

Restoring and refunds

The upcoming unlock includes Restore Purchases. For the current paid app, redownload using the Apple Account that bought it. Apple handles billing and refund requests through Report a Problem. Get support if you cannot access your purchase.

US pricing is a reference. Local currencies, taxes and the final App Store offer may differ. No crossed-out “original price” or countdown offer is implied.
